Sector resources

  • 5W reporting is due by the last working day of every month, covering the month just ended.
  • Use one submission per activity, per location, per reporting month — submit multiple reports for multiple sites.
  • Beneficiary figures should reflect people reached in the current reporting month only, not cumulative totals.
  • Replace this panel with links to your sector's guidance note, HNRP indicator list, and meeting minutes.
